# Logos

Full-color SVGs with their fills inlined on each shape, so they render the same anywhere; place them with `<img>`.

- **Full logomark** — `mombossw-logo-color.svg`, `mombossw-logo-bw.svg`. The complete mark; use where a single hero mark is needed.
- **Brandmark** — `mbw-brandmark-color.svg`, `mbw-brandmark-bw.svg`. The sun-burst symbol alone: app icon, social avatar, small-scale mark.
- **Submark** — `mbw-submark-color.svg`, `mbw-submark-bw.svg`. Brandmark + "MOM BOSS wellness" lockup.
- **Workmark** — `mbw-workmark-color.svg`, `mbw-workmark-bw.svg`. Wordmark only; the `NavBar` logo on light grounds. On `color-deep-grove` or `surface-inverse` use `mbw-workmark-color-social-on-grove.svg` (the `Footer` logo).
- **Social colorways** — `*-social-deep-green.svg`, `*-social-gold.svg`, `*-social-purple.svg`, the square `*-social.svg` crops and `mbw-avatar-ig.svg`, for backgrounds where the base colors don't fit.
- **Submark on dark** — `mbw-submark-social-on-dark.svg`. Transparent submark with ivory lettering and an ivory-edged orchid, for dark grounds (`color-rooted-evergreen`, `color-deep-grove`) in social and digital use only, never print. It is invisible on light grounds. For the square avatar crop on Deep Grove use `mbw-submark-social-on-grove.svg`.
- **Black and white** — `*-bw.svg`, a single white ink (`#fff`) for dark grounds such as `surface-inverse` or a photo; invisible on light grounds.
