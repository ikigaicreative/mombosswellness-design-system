Forty-eight original line icons for the wellness realms of the five mom personas (Running on Empty, Rebuilding, on Pause, Reinventing, and the Mom Who Builds), grouped into nine realms. Select an icon to copy its SVG; the persona buttons show each mom's set of nine.

Draw icons on a 24px grid with a 2px stroke, round caps and joins, in a single color via `currentColor`. Rooted Evergreen is the default; Ivory on `color-rooted-evergreen` or `color-deep-grove`; Rooted Ink on gold.

Files: `assets/icons/svg/mbw-*.svg` (one per icon), `assets/icons/mbw-sprite.svg` (every icon as `<symbol id="mbw-name">`), and `assets/icons/icon-guide.json` (which icons belong to each persona and realm).
